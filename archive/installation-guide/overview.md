> [!INFO]\
>[__Linux Mint__](mint) is a free, open-source operating system designed to work "out of the box." It is a popular alternative for those accustomed to a traditional desktop OS like Windows, offering a simple transition and access to the apps that most people need.
> - It is completely free to install and use.
> - You can "dual boot" and install Linux Mint alongside your computer's existing OS.
